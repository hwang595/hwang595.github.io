abstract: |
  K2-Horizon spans six open language models, from a 0.9B dense model to a mixture-of-experts model with 375B total parameters and 23B active per token. Four models share a 22-trillion-token training corpus, allowing researchers to compare dense and sparse architectures on the same data. The team released intermediate checkpoints, training logs, data pipelines, and code for training and evaluation.
