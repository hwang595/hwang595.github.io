abstract: |
  AIDO Cell introduces a general-purpose cell biology simulator built on a world model that maintains a coherent cell state across sequential experiments. It supports genetic and molecular perturbations, multimodal readouts spanning genome regulation through cell morphology, and in-context molecular design. Version 1.0 presents prototype virtual cells for the K-562 and Hep-G2 cell lines and positions the system as a composable unit toward a complete digital organism.
